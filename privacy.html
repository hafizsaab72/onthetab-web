Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

OnTheTab is a group expense app. This page explains what we collect so the product can work, and what we do not do with it.

Who we are

OnTheTab is operated from India. Questions: hello@onthetab.in.

What we collect

  • Account. Email address, display name, and sign-in data (password, magic link, or Sign in with Google or Apple).
  • Expenses and groups. Amounts, currencies, split details, notes, who paid, and who is in a group or friend pair. That is the product.
  • Receipt photos. Only if you attach one. Photos are stored so people on that expense can view them.
  • Contacts and camera. Used on your device to find friends already on OnTheTab, photograph receipts, or scan a friend QR code. We do not sell your address book.
  • Device signals. Crash reports and, if enabled, product analytics so we can fix bugs and see which screens fail. Push tokens are stored only so we can send the notifications you allow.

Sign in with Google or Apple

You can sign in with Google or with Apple instead of a password. Google LLC and Apple Inc. are the sign-in providers. When you choose one, it confirms who you are and shares with us:

  • Google: your name, email address, profile photo link, and a Google account ID.
  • Apple: your name (only the first time you sign in), your email address or, if you choose Hide My Email, an Apple private relay address that forwards to you, and an Apple account ID.

We use this only to create and sign in to your OnTheTab account and to show your name to people you share expenses with. We do not get your Google or Apple password, and we do not post anything to those accounts. If your Google or Apple email matches an existing OnTheTab account, both sign-ins open the same account.

Why we use it

To run your account, show balances, sync groups with people you invite, send optional notifications, improve reliability, and provide Pro features if you are on the allowlist.

Who can see it

People you add to a group or a 1:1 expense can see that shared activity. We use hosted infrastructure (database, auth, and file storage) to keep the app online. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

While your account is open. If you ask us to delete the account, we remove or de-identify personal data that is not required for a legal record of a shared expense with someone else still using the app.

Your choices

  • You can decline contacts, camera, photos, or notifications in system settings. Core splitting still works without them.
  • You can email hello@onthetab.in to export or delete your account, including one that signs in with Google or Apple. See Support.

Children

OnTheTab is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account for a child.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update this page and the date above.
