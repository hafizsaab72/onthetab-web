Delete your account

Email hello@onthetab.in with the subject Delete my OnTheTab account, from the email address on your account. This works however you sign in: email and password, magic link, Google, or Apple.

  • Signed in with Google: send the request from that Google email address.
  • Signed in with Apple using Hide My Email: your account email is an Apple relay address. Write from any address and tell us the name on your account; we will confirm through the relay address before deleting.

We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email. We remove your sign-in, profile, receipt photos, and push tokens. Expenses you shared with someone who still uses the app stay in their history with your name removed, so their balances still add up.

If you signed in with Google or Apple, you can also remove OnTheTab's access from your Google account (Security → Your connections to third-party apps) or your Apple Account (Sign in with Apple). That stops the sign-in but does not delete your OnTheTab data; email us for that.
